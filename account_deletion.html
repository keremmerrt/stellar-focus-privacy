Account Deletion

Last updated: May 30, 2026

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Your email address and username will be removed from our servers. Local app data (sessions, galaxy, Stardust) can be removed separately inside the app.

What will be deleted

  • Your Stellar Focus account credentials (email address and username) stored on Supabase servers
  • Your authentication session

Note: Your focus session data, galaxy, Stardust balance, missions, and achievements are stored locally on your device only and are not held on our servers. To delete this local data, use Settings → Reset All Data inside the app.

How to request account deletion

Option 1 — Inside the app (fastest)

Open Stellar Focus → go to Settings → tap Request Account Deletion. This will open your email app with a pre-filled deletion request. Send the email and we will process your request within 30 days.

Option 2 — Email us directly

Send an email to stellarfocus.support@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Stellar Focus account deletion request
  • Your account email address
  • A short note confirming you want your account deleted
Send deletion request

We will confirm deletion by replying to your email within 30 days. If you do not receive a reply within that window, please check your spam folder or contact us again.

After deletion

  • You will no longer be able to sign in to Stellar Focus with that account.
  • Local app data on your device will remain unless you also use Reset All Data inside the app.
  • Deletion cannot be undone. If you want to use Stellar Focus again you will need to create a new account.

Contact

Questions? Email stellarfocus.support@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
