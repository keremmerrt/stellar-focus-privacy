Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Stellar Focus is designed to keep your data private. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers. The only personal data we collect is your email address, used solely to create and secure your account.

1. What Data We Collect

Account data (stored on Supabase servers)

When you register, we collect your email address. We use Supabase Authentication to manage login credentials. Passwords are never accessible to Stellar Focus and are securely handled by Supabase — we only receive confirmation that authentication succeeded or failed. Your email is stored on Supabase infrastructure (see Section 4).

App data (stored locally on your device only)

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Focus session records (duration, start time, completion status)
  • Cosmic objects earned from completed sessions (type, tier, position in your galaxy)
  • Stardust currency balance and transaction history
  • Achievement unlock status
  • Mission progress (daily and weekly missions)
  • App preferences and onboarding completion status
  • Streak tracking (consecutive days with completed sessions)

Note: If a future version of Stellar Focus adds cloud backup or cross-device sync, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Email address — used only for account authentication (sign in, sign out, password recovery). We do not use it for marketing without your explicit consent.
  • App data — used entirely on-device to power app features (galaxy, missions, achievements, history). We have no access to this data.

3. App Blocking Feature

Stellar Focus includes a focus-mode toggle that will allow you to block distracting apps during sessions. This feature is not active in version 1.0 and does not access any app usage data. When the feature launches in a future update, it will be Android-only and will require your explicit permission. You will be shown a clear disclosure before any permission is requested. iOS does not support third-party app blocking of this kind.

4. Third-Party Services

Supabase (Authentication)

We use Supabase to manage user accounts. Your email address is stored on Supabase infrastructure, hosted on AWS in the US/EU region. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Stellar Focus does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or crash-reporting services. No data is sold to or shared with any third party beyond Supabase authentication.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Account data: Your email is retained as long as your account exists. To request account deletion, email us at the address below. Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Upon deletion, your account data is permanently removed from Supabase servers.

App data: All local app data (sessions, galaxy, stardust, etc.) can be deleted at any time from the app's Settings screen using "Reset All Data." You can also uninstall the app, which removes all local data from your device.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to or restrict how we process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Stellar Focus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

8. Security

Passwords are managed exclusively by Supabase and are never accessible to us in plain text. Local app data is stored in an SQLite database protected by device-level security (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). We do not implement additional application-level encryption on local data in version 1.0.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
