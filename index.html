Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Stellar Focus is designed to keep your data private. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers. The only personal data we collect is your email address, used solely to create and secure your account.

1. What Data We Collect

Account data (stored on our servers)

When you register, we collect your email address and a hashed version of your password. This is used to authenticate you across devices. Your email is stored on Supabase servers (see Section 4). We do not collect your name, phone number, or any other personal identifiers.

App data (stored locally on your device only)

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Focus session records (duration, start time, completion status)
  • Cosmic objects earned from completed sessions (type, tier, position in your galaxy)
  • Stardust currency balance and transaction history
  • Achievement unlock status
  • Mission progress (daily and weekly missions)
  • App preferences and onboarding completion status
  • Streak tracking (consecutive days with completed sessions)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Email address — used only for account authentication (sign in / sign out / password recovery). We do not use it for marketing without your explicit consent.
  • App data — used entirely on-device to power app features (galaxy, missions, achievements, history). We have no access to this data.

3. App Blocking Feature

Stellar Focus includes a focus-mode toggle that will allow you to block distracting apps during sessions. This feature is not active in version 1.0 and does not access any app usage data. When the feature launches in a future update, it will be Android-only and will require your explicit permission. You will be shown a clear disclosure before any permission is requested.

iOS does not support third-party app blocking of this kind. No usage data is collected on iOS.

4. Third-Party Services

Supabase (Authentication)

We use Supabase to manage user accounts. Your email address and hashed password are stored on Supabase infrastructure, which is hosted on AWS in the US/EU region. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Stellar Focus does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or crash-reporting services. No data is sold to or shared with any third party.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Account data: Your email and hashed password are retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account by contacting us at the email below. Upon deletion, your account data is permanently removed from Supabase servers.

App data: All local app data (sessions, galaxy, stardust, etc.) can be deleted at any time from the app's Settings screen using "Reset All Data." You can also delete the app from your device, which removes all local data.

6. Children's Privacy

Stellar Focus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Local app data is stored in an SQLite database protected by device-level security. We do not implement additional encryption on local data in version 1.0.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
