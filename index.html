Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Stellar Focus is designed to keep your focus-session and galaxy data on your device. If account registration is enabled, we use account information only to create and manage your account. We do not sell or share your personal information.

1. What Data We Collect

Stellar Focus stores focus-session and galaxy data locally on your device. If account registration is enabled, we collect the email address and username you provide to create and manage your account.

Your date of birth is used only on your device to confirm that you are eligible to create an account. Users under 13 cannot create an account. Your date of birth is not stored or transmitted to us.

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Focus session records, such as duration, start and end time, and completion status
  • Cosmic objects earned from completed sessions, such as planet type and position in your galaxy
  • Stardust currency balance and transaction history
  • Achievement unlock status
  • Mission progress, including daily and weekly missions
  • App preferences and onboarding completion status
  • Streak tracking, such as consecutive days with completed sessions
  • Optional blocked-app selections, if you use the app blocking feature

The following data is not transmitted to us:

  • Which apps you have installed on your device
  • Which apps you choose to block during sessions
  • Your date of birth
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, such as advertising ID or IMEI
  • Analytics, advertising, or usage telemetry
  • Focus-session, galaxy, Stardust, achievement, mission, or blocked-app data

2. How We Use Your Data

We do not use your app data on any server because we do not have access to it. The local data listed above stays on your device and is used only to make the app function.

For example:

  • Session records are used to display your galaxy and calculate your streak
  • The Stardust ledger is used to display your currency balance
  • Achievement and mission data is used to show your progress
  • Preferences are used to remember your settings between sessions
  • Blocked-app selections are used locally to support the optional app blocking feature

Account data is used only for account creation and sign-in. Focus-session and galaxy data is not processed on our servers. No personal data is sold or used for advertising.

3. App Blocking Feature

Stellar Focus includes an optional app blocking feature. This feature is only used when you choose to enable it.

Android

The app may request access to usage statistics through Android's "Usage access" system setting. This permission is used to detect when a blocked app comes to the foreground during an active focus session.

This information is used only on your device and only for the purpose of enforcing an active focus session. It is not sent to us, not sold, and not shared with third parties. Your blocked-app list is stored locally on your device and is not accessible to us as developers.

iOS

The app uses Apple's Screen Time framework, including FamilyControls and ManagedSettings. App selection is handled through Apple's FamilyActivityPicker, a system interface designed so that developers cannot see the specific apps selected by the user. We only receive an opaque token from Apple. We do not know which apps you blocked.

4. Third-Party Services

Stellar Focus uses Supabase for account registration and sign-in if accounts are enabled. Stellar Focus v1.0 does not integrate third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or third-party crash reporting SDKs. There are no Firebase Analytics, AdMob, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar integrations in this version.

App stores and operating systems, such as Google Play, Android, iOS, or the App Store, may provide platform-level diagnostic or crash information according to their own privacy settings and policies. Stellar Focus does not add its own third-party analytics or crash reporting service.

If additional third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated. If any future feature causes focus-session or galaxy data to leave your device, we will notify you in-app before that feature is activated and, where required, ask for your consent.

5. Children's Privacy

Stellar Focus is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If account registration is enabled, the date of birth entered during registration is used only on your device to confirm that you are eligible to create an account. Users under 13 cannot create an account. Your date of birth is not stored or transmitted to us.

Because Stellar Focus does not transmit focus-session, galaxy, or birth-date data to us, we do not knowingly receive personal information from children under 13 through the app.

The app is intended for general audiences, especially students and knowledge workers aged 13 and older. It contains no violence, adult content, or inappropriate material.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your app data is stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed, unless you delete it earlier.

You can delete all local app data at any time using one of these methods:

  • In the app: Settings → Reset All Data (deletes all local session, galaxy, Stardust, mission, and achievement data)
  • On Android: Settings → Apps → Stellar Focus → Storage → Clear Data
  • By uninstalling the app

There is no server-side focus-session or galaxy data to delete because that app data is not sent to our servers. Deletion of local data is immediate and irreversible. We have no copy of your local app data and cannot restore it.

Account deletion: To request deletion of your Supabase account (email address and username), use Settings → Request Account Deletion in the app, or email stellarfocus.support@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request." You can also submit a request via our account deletion page. We will process account deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Security

Your app data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. The database is protected by your device's own security model, such as app sandboxing and device-level encryption where available.

We do not apply additional application-level encryption to the local database in this version.

Because your app data stays on your device, the primary security boundary is your device's own lock screen and operating system security. We recommend keeping your device locked and your Android or iOS software up to date.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change.

We will notify you of material changes in-app before they take effect where appropriate.

If we ever add cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or any feature that causes app data to leave your device, that change will not happen silently. We will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, ask for your explicit consent before activating that feature.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Because Stellar Focus does not receive or store your focus-session or galaxy data on our servers, we cannot access, export, correct, or delete your local app data for you. You remain in control of the data stored on your own device.

You can exercise control over your data in the following ways:

Access

You can view your local data inside the app, such as your session history, galaxy, achievements, missions, and preferences.

Deletion

You can delete all local app data at any time through Settings → Reset All Data, through your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. To delete your Supabase account (email and username), use Settings → Request Account Deletion or contact stellarfocus.support@gmail.com.

Portability

Stellar Focus v1.0 does not currently provide a built-in export feature. Your data remains stored locally on your device. We may add an export option in a future version.

Correction

Where the app provides editing features, you can correct supported local data directly inside the app.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties. We do not use app data for advertising or tracking.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Stellar Focus's data practices, please contact:

Email: stellarfocus.support@gmail.com

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry. We do not add it to a marketing list and do not share it with third parties, unless required by law.

We aim to respond to privacy-related inquiries within 5 business days.
