Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Stellar Focus keeps your focus-session and galaxy data on your device. If account registration is enabled, we use account information only to create and manage your account. We do not sell personal information, run ads, or use analytics trackers.

1. What Data We Collect

Account data (stored on Supabase servers)

When you register, we collect the email address and username you provide. We use Supabase Authentication to manage login credentials. Passwords are never accessible to Stellar Focus and are securely handled by Supabase. Your email address is used for sign-in, account security, and account recovery. Your username is used to personalize your account in the app.

Age eligibility check (not stored)

During registration, the date of birth you enter is used only on your device to confirm that you are eligible to create an account. Users under 13 cannot create an account. Your date of birth is not stored locally, not sent to Supabase, and not transmitted to us.

App data (stored locally on your device only)

The following data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Focus session records, including duration, start time, and completion status
  • Cosmic objects earned from completed sessions
  • Stardust currency balance and transaction history
  • Achievement unlock status
  • Mission progress, including daily and weekly missions
  • App preferences and onboarding completion status
  • Streak tracking, such as consecutive days with completed sessions

If a future version adds cloud backup or cross-device sync for focus-session or galaxy data, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Email address — used only for account authentication, account security, and password recovery.
  • Username — used only to personalize your account inside Stellar Focus.
  • App data — used on-device to power app features such as your galaxy, missions, achievements, history, and Stardust balance. We do not have access to this data.

3. App Blocking Feature

Stellar Focus includes a focus-mode toggle for optional app blocking. This feature is not active in version 1.0 and does not access app usage data. When real app blocking launches in a future update, it will require explicit user permission and a clear disclosure before any permission is requested. Blocked-app selections will stay on the device.

4. Third-Party Services

Supabase (Authentication)

We use Supabase to manage user accounts. Account data is stored on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Stellar Focus does not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or third-party crash reporting services. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Account data: Your email address and username are retained while your account exists. To request account deletion, contact us at the email address below. Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Upon deletion, your account data is removed from Supabase according to Supabase's retention and backup practices.

Local app data: Focus sessions, galaxy objects, Stardust, achievements, missions, and preferences can be deleted at any time from the app's Settings screen using "Reset All Data." You can also uninstall the app, which removes local app data from your device.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal data associated with your account. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Stellar Focus accounts are available only to users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

8. Security

Passwords are managed by Supabase and are never accessible to us in plain text. Local app data is stored in the app's local SQLite database and protected by the operating system's app sandbox and device-level security, such as device encryption where available. We do not apply additional application-level encryption to the local database in version 1.0.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
