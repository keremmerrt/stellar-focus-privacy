Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Short version: Stellar Focus stores all your data on your device only. We collect nothing. We have no servers. Nothing leaves your phone.

1. What Data We Collect

Stellar Focus does not collect, transmit, or receive any personal data. There are no user accounts, no registration, and no login. The app works entirely without an internet connection.

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Focus session records — duration, start and end time, completion status
  • Cosmic objects earned from completed sessions (planet type, position in your galaxy)
  • Stardust currency balance and transaction history
  • Achievement unlock status
  • Mission progress (daily and weekly missions)
  • App preferences and onboarding completion status
  • Streak tracking (consecutive days with completed sessions)

The following data is never collected or transmitted:

  • Which apps you have installed on your device
  • Which apps you choose to block during sessions
  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data of any kind
  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, IMEI, etc.)
  • Any analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry
  • Any data sent to our servers — we have no servers in this version

2. How We Use Your Data

We do not use your data because we do not have access to it. All data listed above stays on your device and is used only to make the app function:

  • Session records are used to display your galaxy and calculate your streak
  • The currency ledger is used to display your Stardust balance
  • Achievement and mission data is used to show your progress in those screens
  • Preferences are used to remember your settings between sessions

No data is processed on any server. No data is shared with any third party. No data is sold. No data is used for advertising.

3. App Blocking Feature

Stellar Focus includes an optional app blocking feature. When you use it:

  • Android: The app may request access to usage statistics (via Android's "Usage access" system setting) to detect when a blocked app comes to the foreground. This information is used only in real time during an active session — it is never stored and never transmitted. Your list of blocked apps is stored locally on your device only and is not accessible to us as developers.
  • iOS: The app uses Apple's Screen Time framework (FamilyControls / ManagedSettings). App selection is handled through Apple's own FamilyActivityPicker — a system interface that is specifically designed so that we, the developers, cannot see which apps you have selected. We only receive an opaque token from Apple. We never know which apps you blocked.

4. Third-Party Services

Stellar Focus v1.0 does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash reporting services. There are no Firebase, AdMob, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar integrations in this version.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated and you will be notified in-app before any data leaves your device. You will have the option to opt out.

5. Children's Privacy

Stellar Focus is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app. Because we collect no data at all, there is no risk of inadvertently collecting children's personal information.

The app is rated "Everyone" by Google Play and is appropriate for general audiences. It contains no violence, adult content, or inappropriate material. Its target audience is students and knowledge workers ages 13 and older.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete all app data at any time using one of these methods:

  • In-app: Settings → Delete all my data (immediately and permanently removes all local data)
  • Via Android settings: Settings → Apps → Stellar Focus → Storage → Clear Data
  • By uninstalling the app (removes all local app data)

There is no server-side data to delete because no data is ever sent to a server. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — we have no copy of your data to restore from.

7. Security

Your session data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. The database is protected by your device's own security model (full-disk encryption, app sandboxing). We do not apply additional application-level encryption to the local database in this version.

Because data never leaves your device, the primary security boundary is your device's own lock screen and system security. We recommend keeping your device locked and your Android or iOS software up to date.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the most recent change was made. We will notify you of any material changes in-app before they take effect. Continued use of Stellar Focus after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

If we ever add cloud sync, analytics, or any feature that causes data to leave your device, that change will require explicit in-app consent before it is activated. It will not happen silently via an app update.

9. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because Stellar Focus does not collect or process any personal data on a server, the traditional GDPR and CCPA rights (access, portability, erasure, correction) apply only to data on your own device. You have full control over all your data at all times:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app (session history, galaxy, achievements)
  • Deletion: Delete all data at any time via Settings → Delete all my data
  • Portability: The SQLite database file is stored in your app's documents directory and can be accessed via Android file manager or iOS Files app if needed
  • Correction: Session names can be edited within the app

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties. There is no data controller or data processor relationship because no data is processed outside your device.
